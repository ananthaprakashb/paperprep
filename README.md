# Autonomous Reliability for Cloud-Native Financial Platforms Using Predictive Error Budget Intelligence

Research workspace for a proposed paper on predicting SLO-budget exhaustion and safely taking bounded, reversible corrective actions in high-throughput financial services.

- [Curated authoritative knowledge base](knowledge-base/autonomous_reliability_resources.md)
- [Research questions and experimental blueprint](research/research_blueprint.md)

**Research thesis (not yet demonstrated):** Calibrated risk forecasting plus deterministic safety gates can improve warning lead time and reduce SLO breaches without increasing payment-integrity failures or unnecessary interventions.

Forecasting should advise; approved policy controls whether an action is permissible. Do not autonomously initiate irreversible payments, settlement changes, or ledger edits. Treat timeout-unknown payment outcomes as indeterminate, not a license to retry.
