# PaperPrep — Deterministic Agentic AI Governance

Research and preparation materials for **Deterministic Agentic AI Governance for Mission-Critical Payment Infrastructure**.

## Knowledge base

[Read the source-linked architect's knowledge base](knowledge-base/deterministic_agentic_payment_governance_knowledge_base.md).

The guide covers payment correctness, distributed transactions, AI agents and MCP, delegated authorization, policy enforcement, deterministic execution, resilience, compliance, verification, and research planning.

### Research principle

> AI systems may plan probabilistically, but financial authority, execution, and reconciliation require deterministic, verifiable controls.

### Citation and verification

The knowledge base distinguishes primary standards, engineering documentation, academic research, and proposed architecture. References are curated background sources, **not** evidence that every reference was used in the original architecture. Confirm normative versions and applicability before publication or compliance claims.
